Crafted with Precision

Built for Performance. Designed to Disappear.

Every detail in ORBIT is tuned for speed, unobtrusive elegance, and deep native operating system integration on Windows and Mac.

Color-Coded Dynamic Alerts

The Dynamic Island automatically pulses in the app's brand accent: vibrant emerald green for WhatsApp messages, royal blue for system alerts, and warm amber for notifications.

Synchronized Lyrics & Aura

Tracks Spotify, Apple Music, and media players in real-time. Features album color aura extraction that dynamically transitions between tracks, plus millisecond-accurate synchronized lyrics.

Full Soundcore & Bluetooth Telemetry

Reads left and right earbud batteries individually plus the charging case battery. Toggle Noise Cancelling, Transparency, Normal mode, and Spatial 3D Audio right from the island.

Drag & Drop Clipboard Shelf

Drag any text, image, or snippet to the island to pin it. Drag it back out to any application whenever you need it. Includes one-click clear and automatic history management.

Native Windows & Mac Auto-Start

Toggle "Start ORBIT with System" directly from the built-in Settings tab. Uses native OS startup registration — no scheduled tasks, no UAC prompts, and no background daemon bloat.

100% Offline & Private

Zero telemetry, zero user tracking, zero external network calls. All lyrics, audio states, and system statistics are parsed locally on your device.
